CCTV Vision Detection

NODI-Seconds uses the existing CCTV camera at each junction to visually confirm the ambulance — no extra sensors on the vehicle needed.

What the Camera Sees

A small vision program analyzes the CCTV feed in real-time. It identifies the ambulance's distinctive shape — its rectangular body, light bar on top, and flashing emergency lights — separating it from regular traffic, two-wheelers, and buses.

Why CCTV Over Optical Sensors

Optical sensors only detect light flashes — they cannot distinguish an ambulance strobe from a police beacon or decorative LED. CCTV vision confirms the vehicle type AND the lights together, making detection far more reliable and eliminating false triggers.
